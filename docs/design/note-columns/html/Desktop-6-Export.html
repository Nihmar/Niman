Monte Baldo

The ridge from the south, from the Ferrara road.

FactValue
Highest pointCima Valdritta, 2,218 m
RangeGarda Prealps
Cable carMalcesine, 1,760 m

Monte Baldo is the long ridge on the eastern shore of Lake Garda, some forty kilometres from Malcesine to the Adige valley. Botanists call it hortus Europae.

Go early
The first car leaves Malcesine at 8:00.

The crest path runs from the top station to Cima Valdritta in about three hours.

Seasons

Spring
Orchids on the Bocca di Navene; snow above 1,800 m until May.
Summer
Start before eight; water at Rifugio Telegrafo only.
Autumn
The clearest views of the year; the larches turn mid-October.

A column’s flex-grow is its share; over a basis of 0 the room after the gaps is split by share, and the 16 em minimum makes a column that does not fit wrap — the app’s own rule. The seasons wrap two and one in both: three columns of 16 em and two gaps are wider than the 600 px the page gives its text at 15 px, and wider than the 174 mm of an A4 page at 11 pt, where the 40 % / 60 % pair still fits side by side.
