Monte Baldo

The ridge from the south, from the Ferrara road.

FactValue
Highest pointCima Valdritta, 2,218 m
RangeGarda Prealps
Cable carMalcesine, 1,760 m

Monte Baldo is the long ridge on the eastern shore of Lake Garda, some forty kilometres from Malcesine to the Adige valley. Botanists call it hortus Europae: the ice left its summits uncovered, and plants survived there that grow nowhere else.

Go early

The first car leaves Malcesine at 8:00. By ten the queue reaches the lake road.

The crest path runs from the top station to Cima Valdritta in about three hours. #hiking

Getting there

From Verona, the A22 to Affi and the lake road north to Malcesine; the cable car climbs in two stages.

Columns

Toolbar: Columns (Icons.view_column_outlined) in the Insert group, after Table (ToolbarItem.columns), hovered here with its tooltip. Context menu: Insert › Columns, after Table. Palette: Insert columns, with no default shortcut, as Insert diagram has none. All three write the same two-column template at the caret’s block (Desktop-5), with a blank line before it when the line above is text, as a table is inserted. On a phone the same button rides the keyboard, and the Insert sheet lists Columns (Phone-2).
