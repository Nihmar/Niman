# Monte Baldo
 
:::: columns
::: {.column width="40%"}
![[baldo-map.png]]
The ridge from the south, from the Ferrara road.
 
| Fact | Value |
|---|---|
| Highest point | Cima Valdritta, 2,218 m |
| Range | Garda Prealps |
| Cable car | Malcesine, 1,760 m |
:::
 
::: column
Monte Baldo is the long ridge on the eastern shore of [[Lake Garda]], some forty kilometres from Malcesine to the Adige valley. Botanists call it *hortus Europae*: the ice left its summits uncovered, and plants survived there that grow nowhere else.
 
> [!tip] Go early
> The first car leaves Malcesine at 8:00. By ten the queue reaches the lake road.
 
The crest path runs from the top station to Cima Valdritta in about three hours. #hiking
:::
::::
 
## Getting there
From Verona, the A22 to Affi and the lake road north.
