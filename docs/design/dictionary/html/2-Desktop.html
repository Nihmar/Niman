Chapter 1 — The Psychopathology of Everyday Things

Well-designed objects are easy to interpret and understand. They contain visible clues to their operation. Poorly designed objects can be difficult and frustrating to use.

The same technology that simplifies life by providing more functions in each device also complicates life by making the device harder to learn, harder to use. This is the paradox of technology, and it is ubiquitous in the modern world of gadgets.

Two of the most important characteristics of good design are discoverability and understanding.
