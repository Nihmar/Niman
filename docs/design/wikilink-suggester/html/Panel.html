Wikilink suggester

While a wikilink is being typed, a small panel lists what can go there and filters as the text grows. 1 to 4 are the states the issue describes, 5 the book target, 6 the same panel over the source. Each is drawn in the app's window, most again larger, because the panel is too small to judge at arm's length.

These are the decided shapes: the maintainer answered the three questions the drawing was made to ask (Decisions, 2026-09-28, on #475). The panel appears in the live editor, where the caret is and where the link is written, and in source too — the same panel, the same rows, over the raw text.

Rows read name then the folder, dimmed, so a link can name the right one. A row found through an alias carries the alias: alias md. The matched run is in bold: Links, Dead links. Keys: ↑↓ move, ↵ or Tab insert, Esc close.

4 — nothing matches · the decided shape, and the row decided against

4a is the decided shape: the panel says nothing matched and offers nothing. 4b adds the New note row that question 1 asked for — drawn crossed out, because the answer was no: the panel lists only what exists. A note nobody has written is made from the dead link itself, on ctrl+click — see 4c.

4c — where the note comes from · ctrl+click on the dead link

The panel offers nothing, so the link itself is the affordance: a wikilink that names no note is a dead link, and ctrl+click on one creates the note it names and opens it (#477). The panel never writes it.

5 — a book target · the decided # form: pages and chapters

Books are in scope: when the link's target resolves to a PDF or an EPUB, the # completion offers its pages and chapters — [[Dune.pdf#page=34]] and [[Dune.epub#chapter=…&line=12]], the forms docs/user/links.md documents. These are the decided shapes, not a probe.

This state was drawn as a probe to settle question 3, leaning that a book had "no list to offer", only a page= form to type. The decision went the other way: books are in scope, and the completion offers their pages and chapters. A PDF's pages are still numbers to type, but an EPUB's named chapters are a list to filter like any other.

6 — in source too · the same panel over the raw text

The panel is not the live editor's alone. The decision put it in source as well: over the raw text, the same rows and the same keys, positioned from the caret just as in the live editor.
