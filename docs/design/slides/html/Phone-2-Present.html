A tap on the right third goes forward, on the left third back; a swipe left/right too. A swipe down exits to the slide view — which, with the phone still sideways, stays in the slide view until the phone turns upright and sideways again. The faint counter is the only chrome. A Bluetooth clicker works as is: it sends Page Up / Page Down, the keys the desktop already answers.
