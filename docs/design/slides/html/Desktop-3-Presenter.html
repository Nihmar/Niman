The timer starts on the first move after the view opens, not on opening it; P pauses it, R restarts it. The keys are the presenting ones. Slide only goes back to the bare slide (F5), Exit to the slide view. Long notes scroll in their own column; the slides keep their place. On the last slide, Next is disabled rather than hidden.
