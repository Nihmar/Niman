← → (and Page Up / Page Down) move between slides while the pane has focus; a click on a thumbnail jumps there. The Present button beside the pencil is F5. The pencil opens the raw editor, where the kind toggle offers the slides back. A slide is laid out at 960×540 and scaled to the pane, so it looks the same in the pane, on the projector and in the PDF. No speaker notes in a slide → no notes box (the slide moves up; nothing else moves).
