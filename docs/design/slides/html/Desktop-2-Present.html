Slides — presenting on the desktop

F5 (or Present) takes the whole screen for the slides alone. The slide is the 960×540 layout scaled to the screen, letterboxed in black where the screen is not 16:9.

No chrome, no cursor. → ↓ Space Page Down and a click advance; ← ↑ Backspace Page Up go back; Home / End jump to the first / last; Esc gives the screen back to the slide view, on the slide where the talk stopped. B blacks the screen out and back, as every presentation app does.
