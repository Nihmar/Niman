1.2 Subspaces and linear dependence

Once a coordinate system is chosen, the geometric vectors of space can be identified with ℝ³, and the operations between vectors become operations on triples.

Definition 1.2.1. A non-empty subset W of ℝ³ is a subspace if it is closed under addition and under multiplication by a scalar.

⟨v⟩ = { αv | α ∈ ℝ }

Definition 1.2.2. Given k vectors v₁, …, vₖ, the span is the set of their linear combinations. See [[1.3 Bases and dimension]].
