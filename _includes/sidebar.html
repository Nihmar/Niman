{%- comment -%} The docs sidebar: every page under `docs/user/`, `docs/dev/` and `docs/records/`, grouped the way the documentation is grouped. The guides are listed in the order they are written in — the order of `index.md` "The user guide" for `docs/user/`, of its "Building and changing Niman" list for `docs/dev/`, and of the table in `docs/records/README.md` for the records. A page that is not in one of those lists (a record added without a table row, say) is still shown, after the listed ones, so the sidebar is driven by `site.pages` and nothing can go missing. {%- endcomment -%}
