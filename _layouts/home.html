Niman Is More (than) A Notebook.

A multiplatform Markdown note-taking app where your notes are just files. One note = one .md file on disk — no lock-in, no proprietary format.

What Niman does

What the app does today, in its own words from the README. What is planned is in the issue tracker.

Notes as files

One note = one .md file on disk, organized in folders however you like. The database is only a rebuildable index; your files are the source of truth.

One surface, three modes

The source editor, the live (WYSIWYG) editor and the read view are one widget of Niman's own; switching modes does not move the page.

Notes of other kinds

A note's type: makes it a checklist or a shopping list, a kanban board, a flashcard deck studied with spaced repetition, or slides presented full screen — and the file stays plain Markdown.

Links that reach anywhere

Wikilinks and Markdown links between notes, into a page of a PDF or a chapter of a book, with a dialog to create a missing note.

Journal, tasks and reminders

One note per day with a calendar and the day's events from calendar.txt; a todo list over todo.txt; tasks and events that recur; reminders that fire with the app closed on Android, macOS and iOS, and from the tray on Linux and Windows.

Export, your way

A note, a folder or the whole library out as Markdown, a self-contained HTML page, a print-ready PDF or an EPUB book.

Notes in from anywhere

Capture a web page or a passage of one, convert HTML pages and books or an EPUB into notes, import a Notion export, or open an Obsidian vault as it stands.

Sync to your own server

Per-library WebDAV sync to Nextcloud, ownCloud, a NAS or any WebDAV folder, with an offline queue that retries by itself.

A library of your own

Each library is a folder with its settings inside it: a Home of tiles and actions for what you come back to, and a Features switchboard that turns off what it does not use, with nothing deleted.

Make it yours

Day, night or system brightness, and a palette — System, Niman, Catppuccin, Solarized, Gruvbox, Sepia — or a theme of your own, exported as JSON.

Pictures, PDFs and books

Pictures, PDFs and EPUB books open in the note pane on every platform, zoomed and paged where they need it, opening where you left off; a passage you annotate becomes a quote in a companion note.

Text and speech, on the device

Text recognition turns a scanned PDF or a picture into a note that search indexes, and a voice note's recording is transcribed — both on the device, with nothing uploaded.

Search that scales

Full-text search over titles, bodies and tags with SQLite FTS5, prefix words, key = value fields and #tags — built for a library of a million notes.

Android, Linux, Windows, macOS

Niman runs on Android (minSdk 30, Android 11), Linux (Wayland) and Windows, and on macOS (14 and later) in preview, with a .dmg and a .zip in each release. iOS and iPadOS (17 and later) run but are not released yet.
